Today was the beginning of a journey that is a once-in-a-lifetime event. As I sit in my bedroom, I am more than a little bit nervous about the next few months. Going from school and work to days filled with nothing but the sounds of nature and my two feet is pretty daunting. All of my gear is prepped and ready to go in my room. I'm excited to get out there and start putting miles behind me.

Despite trying my very hardest to have a really light pack, it's still much heavier than I had hoped it would be... stay tuned to hear about the gear that gets left behind.
